Run Kafka Inspector on your own Kafka

Same app, on your server, reading your real cluster. Nothing you type here leaves your browser.
  1. 1Describe your cluster
  2. 2Download and fill in secrets
  3. 3Run docker compose up
AccessRead-only (v1)
Using Amazon MSK, Confluent Cloud or Aiven? See provider settings
services:
  kafka-inspector:
    image: ghcr.io/<owner>/kafka-inspector:1.0.0
    ports:
      - "8080:8080"
    env_file: .env
    environment:
      KI_CLUSTERS_0_NAME: prod-us-2
      KI_CLUSTERS_0_BOOTSTRAP: b-1.prod-us-2:9096,b-2.prod-us-2:9096
      KI_CLUSTERS_0_SECURITY: SASL_SSL
      KI_CLUSTERS_0_SASL_MECHANISM: SCRAM-SHA-512
      KI_CLUSTERS_0_SCHEMA_REGISTRY: https://schema-registry.prod-us-2.internal
      KI_READ_ONLY: "true"
    volumes:
      - ki-data:/data      # metric history and alert rules
volumes:
  ki-data:
