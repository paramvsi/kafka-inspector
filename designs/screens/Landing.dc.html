What it does

Answers the three questions you open a Kafka UI for.

01 · Is anything falling behind?

Lag you can read at a glance

Every consumer group gets a health status (Healthy, Warn, Stalled, Stopped) and a forecast like “catches up in ~14 min” or “won't catch up at current rates”.

02 · What's in this topic?

Find any message, decoded

Live tail, JavaScript filters, search across topics, and automatic decoding of JSON, Avro and JSON Schema through your Schema Registry. Bad messages show their raw bytes instead of disappearing.

value.total > 500 && headers.region === 'eu'
03 · How does data flow?

The whole pipeline on one map

Producers, topics and consumer groups drawn as a live graph. Line width is throughput; colour is health. A stalled consumer turns red before your users notice.

web-checkout→orders→payments-sink

On your server

Self-host against your own Kafka

One Docker container serves the app and talks to your brokers. Works with Apache Kafka 3.x–4.x, Amazon MSK, Confluent Cloud and Aiven. Read-only in version 1.

docker run -p 8080:8080 \
  -e KAFKA_BOOTSTRAP=broker:9092 \
  -e KI_ADMIN_PASSWORD=change-me \
  ghcr.io/<owner>/kafka-inspector:1.0.0
